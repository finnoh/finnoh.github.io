## Abstract

Generative AI shows considerable potential for ideation, yet existing research focuses largely on isolated agents rather than the coordinated teams through which firms organize strategic innovation. We introduce the agentic ideation assemblage, a configurable framework for organizing GenAI agents into strategically aligned ideation teams. Drawing on assemblage theory and research on human ideation, we define agents through four building blocks: resources, personas, activities, and interactions, and coordinate them across governance, operational, and facilitating layers. We operationalize the framework in a multi-agent system and compare its ideas with those from a single agent, using evaluations by senior US retail managers. Ideas receiving the highest ratings for dimensions such as impact and feasibility predominantly come from the multi-agent system. Beyond practical guidance for deploying GenAI in strategic ideation, the assemblage offers a platform for simulated experiments that examine how team composition, organizational culture, and strategic orientation may influence different dimensions of idea quality across organizational settings.
