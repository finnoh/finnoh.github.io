## Abstract

Generative AI makes candidate marketing texts abundant, but market researchers have limited tools to translate findings from prior text evaluations into systematic refinements and candidates for further testing. The authors introduce the *Text-Generating Embedder*, which learns low-dimensional *text-generating embeddings* (TGEs): corpus-specific coordinates that both regenerate observed texts through a fixed large language model and serve as inputs to quantitative outcome models. By linking textual variation to observed outcomes, researchers can identify promising directions for refinement, generate corresponding text variants, and prioritize candidates for subsequent validation. Applications to commercial product claims and vaccination reminders demonstrate how TGEs capture meaningful textual variation, enable directed generation of new candidates, and translate findings from previous text evaluations into concrete wording for follow-up testing.
